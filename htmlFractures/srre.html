SR:REFORMATTED, (acronymized and previously known only as SR:RE) is a series on Scratch made by me. It's based off the original eras of the Starrlian Rangers by Carmine (she makes some cool stuff, go check them out!). Speaking of her actually, she made her own project for this called GENESIS! It's kind of where this all became a series really..

This series' basic premise basically revolves around a group named "The Scratch Resistance," looking to protect the world of Scratch and reunite the world again! Lead by Woody and Gar, they go all around the world to stop evil villains and rebuild the lost stability created from Gorf's black hole gun!

Currently, SR:REFORMATTED is undergoing a second reboot, hopefully soon with more progress than the first two eras. Expect to see it sometime in 2027.

History

Original Run (Timeline 1, 12 March 2025 - 20 December 2025; Timeline 2, debatably 2 April 2026 - 7 July 2026)

SR:REFORMATTED originally started out as just some simple series of assignment remakes! At first, I released "I FINALLY DID ASSIGNMENT 1" and "INVASION!" just doing my own spin on my older submissions. Soon, Carmine struck some interest in these remakes and made GENESIS (Original Version), and that was around the time I made the studio for SR:RE.

If I remember correctly, I was watching a lot of Obsolete Battle Show when I was making a decent chunk of SR:RE, and once I saw GENESIS for the first time I felt as if I owed something to pay back. The original plan for SR:RE was to be a catalyst for the ultimate goal of causing chaos in the CCU (Cosmics Canonical Universe). It was made around the time I found out about the CCU, and I wanted to play around with it a lot... so throughout Floating Mall Quite Tall and Code Haul, I would begin setting the stage for SR:RE becoming an unstable place close to exploding. Code Haul's release was also around when GENESIS got remade as the idea of SR:RE legitimately being Rough Start canonically was retconned.

Now unfortunately, this would be where the original run ends. A few other projects and a vague idea of how the original would've ended were planned and I still have the scripts, so I'll list what would've happened here, since they're not becoming canon anyway.
NOTICE: The reason Original Run has two timeframes is mainly because even though the original run should've ended on the 20th of December 2025, TECHNICALLY, a full reboot was never considered until around the 7th of July (the day I announced Assignment 3 was getting rewritten).

Scrapped Projects (Timeline 1)

Derailment

The only original SR:RE scrapped project to have progress and have a decent chunk shown to the public.

Derailment would've been a remake of Floating Mall Quite Tall. You see, Floating Mall Quite Tall is probably one of my favorite SR:RE projects ever personally. However, there were two main issues with FMQT.

  1. Code Haul did something funny with Diego (the character) and... practically the whole series in general. For starters, Diego (the character) was replaced by Oily because I wasn't comfortable leaving them as is. Code Haul also pretty much overhauled the artstyle and solidified a serious tone, so there's that too. However, for Assignment 1-2 and FMQT, this meant that there was a jarring shift in the whole series. FMQT didn't have it as bad because it did try with its backgrounds, but the underlying issues were still there.
  2. Floating Mall Quite Tall was made during a time where I was still wasn't scripting anything AND trying to establish a world to do stuff in. While this would've been fine if all the major elements from it were kept consistent, as I started to plan the rest of SR:RE out, a lot of what I wanted the world to be completely shifted.

I mainly wanted to remake FMQT because I wanted everything else to stay consistent, and this was the most crucial one to change since it wasn't just an assignment. I also wanted to do more with the mall, so.. I did the greenlight on this project.

Remember when I said that Code Haul overhauled the artstyle? While it was just the backgrounds, the backgrounds got a lot more detailed and took way longer to make. This was pretty much the reason for cancellation, as it took obscenely long to actually make each one and I got really burnt out from working on this project alone.

Main Changes from Floating Mall Quite Tall

  • Diego (the character) replaced by Oily
  • Mall Gang (oh and the mall itself) got extended! Welcome Mark, Bobbi, and... some Piggy Bank..! (Designs were made, just not shown in the publicly released cancelled version.)
  • Ending Scene would've hinted at something going on with Gar.

0xA

0xA would've been a direct continuation of Code Haul, picking up where we left off with the floating mall that was, indeed, quite tall. 0xA would've been split into two plots. Plot A would've seen the mall gang crashland in the desert (thanks to that weird glitch blob from Code Haul) and getting seperated. Plot B would've seen the Resistance recovering from the unprecedented attack by Gorf and his army, while introducing S and Onix. Gorf would've had something to do here as well, but there wasn't really anything written before the initial cancellation of SR:RE.

0xA never made it past the scripting phase and was seemingly scrapped before cancellation, so no progress was made on the project itself.

2026 Reboot / Reboot 1 (7 July 2026 - 18 October 2026)

Admittedly, this didn't really go on for too long. While not a single project was released in full, this version of SR:RE WAS planned out all the way to a tiny portion of Assignment 3!

This was a softer reboot to SR:RE, where the structure of how the series would go stayed the same (GENESIS => Assignment 1-2 => Break => Assignment 3 Saga), but every project was rewritten to accommodate the current changes easier. This was inevitably cancelled because I wanted to branch SR:RE out a bit, and by the time I decided against it I felt as if I wasn't happy with the current structure anyway.
