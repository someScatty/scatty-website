SR:REFORMATTED, (acronymized and previously known only as SR:RE) is a series on Scratch made by me. It's based off the original eras of the Starrlian Rangers by Carmine (she makes some cool stuff, go check them out!). Speaking of her actually, she made her own project for this called GENESIS! It's kind of where this all became a series really..

This series' basic premise basically revolves around a group named "The Scratch Resistance," looking to protect the world of Scratch and reunite the world again! Lead by Woody and Gar, they go all around the world to stop evil villains and rebuild the lost stability created from Gorf's black hole gun!
